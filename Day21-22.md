### Main Lesson

These rooms reinforced the core CTF methodology:

```text
Enumerate
   ↓
Investigate
   ↓
Find a clue
   ↓
Form a hypothesis
   ↓
Test it
   ↓
Gain access
   ↓
Enumerate again
   ↓
Escalate privileges
   ↓
Capture the flag
```

The goal is not to memorize walkthroughs. The goal is to become comfortable following evidence from one discovery to the next.
